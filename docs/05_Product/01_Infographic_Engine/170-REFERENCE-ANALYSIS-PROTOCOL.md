# Reference Analysis Protocol

## 1. Registration
Record stable ID, source filename, source type, origin note and reason for study.

## 2. Structural observation
Describe only visible evidence: canvas, regions, reading order, alignment, visual anchor, whitespace, density and image/text balance.

## 3. Component observation
Identify reusable functional patterns such as title blocks, cards, annotations, callouts, diagrams and footers.

## 4. Visual behavior
Record typography, color, borders, radius, shadows, spacing, illustration and iconography.

## 5. Strengths and weaknesses
Separate useful, problematic and context-dependent patterns.

## 6. Candidate decisions
Convert relevant observations into proposals with rationale, scope and confidence.

## 7. Human approval
No proposal becomes reusable without explicit approval.
